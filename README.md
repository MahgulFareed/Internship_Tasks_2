Task 1 - Bank Marketing Prediction
In this task, I worked with a bank marketing dataset to predict whether a customer would subscribe to a term deposit after a marketing campaign.
What I did:
•	Loaded and explored the dataset
•	Checked for missing values and duplicates
•	Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA)
•	Visualized customer demographics and campaign-related features
•	Encoded categorical variables
•	Trained classification models to predict subscription outcomes
•	Evaluated models using classification metrics
What I found:
•	Certain customer and campaign features strongly influence subscription decisions
•	Data visualization helped identify important trends and patterns
•	The trained model achieved good predictive performance
File: Task1_Bank_Marketing_Prediction_final_Sub.ipynb

Task 2 - Customer Segmentation
In this task, I used customer shopping data to segment customers into different groups based on their purchasing behavior.
What I did:
•	Performed data cleaning and preprocessing
•	Analyzed customer demographics and spending behavior
•	Encoded categorical variables such as Gender
•	Applied feature scaling
•	Used K-Means clustering for customer segmentation
•	Visualized clusters using scatter plots and cluster analysis
What I found:
•	Customers can be grouped into distinct segments based on income and spending patterns
•	High-income customers do not always have high spending scores
•	Segmentation can help businesses design targeted marketing strategies
File: Task2_Customer_Segmentation_Final_Sub.ipynb
